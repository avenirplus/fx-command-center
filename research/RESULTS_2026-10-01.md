### USD/JPY — T2 H4 structure + confirmed H4 Fibonacci pullback + H1 MACD Histogram hidden divergence

Definition:

1. H4 trend mode T2:
   - Long: last two confirmed H4 swing highs and lows are both rising.
   - Short: last two confirmed H4 swing highs and lows are both falling.
   - H4 pivots use L=2 confirmation with conservative no-lookahead availability.
2. Identify the latest confirmed H4 impulse A→B in the trend direction.
3. H1 pulls back into the **50.0–61.8%** retracement zone.

8. One open position per symbol; overlapping signals are ignored.

The raw p-value is **not treated as confirmatory evidence** because multiple assets, filters, trend modes and exits have been inspected. The confidence interval is wide. The sample is only 34 trades over roughly twelve years.

### Implementation audit

The current research implementation was checked for major look-ahead/execution-logic errors:

- H4 EMA trend is shifted one completed H4 bar before use.
- Centered L=2 H4 pivots are not made available until a conservative 12h after the left-labeled pivot bar.
- H1 hidden-divergence pivots are used only after the required two right-side bars are complete.
- Entry is the next H1 open after the signal bar.
- Same-bar TP/SL ambiguity is resolved against the strategy: SL is checked first.
- Same-symbol overlapping positions are blocked until the prior trade exits.
- Time-stop exits are marked at the final allowed bar close in R units.

No major look-ahead flaw was identified in this audit. This does **not** resolve the remaining Bid-only execution limitation.

### Research conclusion for this branch

The deep-pullback + RSI hidden-divergence concept shows a real-looking conditional edge in several markets, but:

- it fails on EUR/USD,
- frequency is low for T3,
- historical spread is material relative to 1 ATR risk,
- USD/JPY and Gold still need full timestamp-aligned Bid/Ask execution,
- results have been inspected across multiple variants.

**Classification: PROMISING SECONDARY STRATEGY FAMILY — NOT ADOPTED FOR LIVE TRADING.**

Do not exclude EUR/USD solely because it lost in this test and then report the remaining markets as an unbiased portfolio. Any market-selection rule must be specified before a new independent validation set is evaluated.

## Documented Method A — H4 20-bar breakout + EMA200

Source specification recovered from the project design:
- H4 confirmed close above the prior 20 H4 highs and above EMA200 -> long candidate.
- H4 confirmed close below the prior 20 H4 lows and below EMA200 -> short candidate.
- Enter at the next available price.
- Initial stop = H4 ATR14 × 2.
- Long exit: confirmed H4 close breaks below the opposite prior-10-bar channel; short is symmetric.
- Channel exit executes at the next available price.
- One position per pair; no averaging.
- Weekend handling was not fixed in the original document. This test used **weekend holding allowed**.
- No parameter rescue was performed after seeing results.

Historical H1 exports were aggregated to H4. A one-spread P/L deduction using each export's SPREAD field was also applied as an approximation; this is not full Ask-path reconstruction.

| Market | Trades | Raw PF | Spread-adjusted PF | Adjusted Avg R | Adjusted Max DD |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| USD/JPY | 463 | 1.088 | **1.038** | +0.021 | 24.8R |
| EUR/USD | 476 | 0.916 | **0.866** | -0.076 | 50.7R |
| GBP/USD | 467 | 0.992 | **0.946** | -0.030 | 48.5R |
| AUD/USD | 490 | 0.833 | **0.776** | -0.126 | 64.5R |

USD/JPY is only marginally above PF 1 after spread deduction and is below PF 1 in 2023–2026 (about 0.955). The other three markets are negative over the full sample after spread deduction.

**Classification: REJECT CURRENT METHOD A SPECIFICATION.**

Do not tune EMA length, breakout length, ATR multiplier, or exit channel after this result and then treat the same data as independent validation.
