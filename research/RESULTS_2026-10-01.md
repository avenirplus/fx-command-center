### USD/JPY — T2 H4 structure + confirmed H4 Fibonacci pullback + H1 MACD Histogram hidden divergence

Definition:

1. H4 trend mode T2:
   - Long: last two confirmed H4 swing highs and lows are both rising.
   - Short: last two confirmed H4 swing highs and lows are both falling.
   - H4 pivots use L=2 confirmation with conservative no-lookahead availability.

### Implementation audit

The current research implementation was checked for major look-ahead/execution-logic errors:

- H4 EMA trend is shifted one completed H4 bar before use.
- Centered L=2 H4 pivots are not made available until a conservative 12h after the left-labeled pivot bar.
- H1 hidden-divergence pivots are used only after the required two right-side bars are complete.
- Entry is the next H1 open after the signal bar.
- Same-bar TP/SL ambiguity is resolved against the strategy: SL is checked first.
- Same-symbol overlapping positions are blocked until the prior trade exits.
- Time-stop exits are marked at the final allowed bar close in R units.

No major look-ahead flaw was identified in this audit. This does **not** resolve the remaining Bid-only execution limitation.
