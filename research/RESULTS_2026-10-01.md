## Current research leader

### USD/JPY — T2 H4 structure + confirmed H4 Fibonacci pullback + H1 MACD Histogram hidden divergence

Definition:

1. H4 trend mode T2:
   - Long: last two confirmed H4 swing highs and lows are both rising.
   - Short: last two confirmed H4 swing highs and lows are both falling.
   - H4 pivots use L=2 confirmation with conservative no-lookahead availability.
2. Identify the latest confirmed H4 impulse A→B in the trend direction.
3. H1 pulls back into the **50.0–61.8%** retracement zone.
4. Pullback magnitude must be at least 0.75 ATR.
5. H1 resumes in the H4 direction.
6. H1 price structure maintains HL/LH and MACD Histogram forms strict hidden divergence.
7. Entry on the next H1 bar.
8. One open position per symbol; overlapping signals are ignored.
9. SL = 1 ATR.
10. Max hold = 48 hours.

### Small-sample diagnostic

At TP 2R the 34 trades correspond to 18 wins and 16 losses.

- Observed win rate: 52.94%
- Zero-cost break-even win rate for 2R: 33.33%
- Raw one-sided binomial diagnostic: p = 0.0143
- Wilson 95% interval for win rate: 36.74%–68.55%

The raw p-value is **not treated as confirmatory evidence** because multiple assets, filters, trend modes and exits have been inspected. The confidence interval is wide. The sample is only 34 trades over roughly twelve years.

**Research classification: PROVISIONAL LEADER — NOT ADOPTED.**

## Why the proper H4 swing test matters

Earlier discovery work approximated Fibonacci depth using a rolling 30-hour H1 range. That proxy produced different conclusions, especially on Gold.

The current validation instead anchors retracement to a **confirmed H4 impulse swing A→B**. Results from the rolling-range proxy must therefore not be promoted to final strategy evidence.

## Additional robustness checks

### Fibonacci depth split

A formal band comparison was run using the same non-overlap, TP 2R framework.

For **USD/JPY T2 + MACD Histogram hidden divergence**:

| Fibonacci band | Trades | PF | Avg R |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 38.2–50.0% | 42 | **0.485** | -0.4048 |
| 50.0–61.8% | 34 | **2.250** | +0.5882 |
| 38.2–61.8% combined | 74 | **1.042** | +0.0270 |

The deep 50–61.8% zone is therefore not interchangeable with the shallower 38.2–50% zone in this implementation.

This matters because the 50–61.8% zone was identified first in the GBP/USD discovery work and then carried into USD/JPY validation. It was not selected by searching USD/JPY for the best Fibonacci sub-band after seeing the USD/JPY result.

For **Gold**, the same depth behavior did not transfer cleanly:

- T1 + MACD hidden, 38.2–50%: 70 trades, PF 1.500
- T1 + MACD hidden, 50–61.8%: 29 trades, PF 1.222
- T1 + MACD hidden, 38.2–61.8%: 97 trades, PF 1.345

Gold therefore appears to have a different depth profile and should not be forced into the USD/JPY rule.

### Parameter-neighborhood sensitivity

A 27-cell diagnostic varied:

- Fibonacci band: broad 45–65%, base 50–61.8%, narrow 52–62%
- Pullback threshold: 0.50 / 0.75 / 1.00 ATR
- H1 hidden-divergence pivot confirmation: L=1 / 2 / 3

For USD/JPY:

- PF>1 in 18/27 cells before synthetic cost
- PF>1 in 15/27 cells after 0.05R synthetic cost
- Median PF: 1.090
- Median PF after 0.05R cost: 1.010
- L=1–2 around the 50–61.8% zone remained strong
- L=3 collapsed below PF 1 across the tested bands
- The 0.50/0.75/1.00 ATR pullback threshold did not change the selected trades in this grid, indicating that threshold is effectively redundant once the other conditions are satisfied in this sample.

For Gold T2 + MACD hidden:

- PF>1 in 21/27 cells
- Median PF: 1.200
- However the canonical T2 + deep-band setup is chronologically unstable, with 2023–2026 PF 0.462.

Interpretation:

- USD/JPY shows **cross-market validation of the deep-band concept**, but sensitivity to the H1 pivot definition remains a real concern.
- Gold shows a smoother parameter surface in this grid but fails the more important chronological-stability test for the same T2 deep-band rule.
- Neither result changes the live-trading status: **no strategy is adopted yet**.
